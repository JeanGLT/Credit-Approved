You are a junior credit analyst.

  1. Read the case documents. Something in them doesn't add up.
  2. Test your own assumptions in the control center. The bank only lends at a DSCR of 1.30x or more.
  3. Send a proposal to the committee: approve, approve with conditions, or decline.
  4. Your decisions move three gauges: bank revenue, portfolio risk and your credibility.
